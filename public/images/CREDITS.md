# Botanical & Historical Photography — Source and Licence

None of these are Baba Ayurved's proprietary brand photography. They are authentic botanical and utensil references sourced from Wikimedia Commons under their stated open licences (CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA), chosen for genuine relevance to classical Ayurvedic dinacharya, ratricharya, rasayana, and Central Indian ethnobotany. They are treated with a refined, warm duotone wash in `components/ui/ImageFrame` so the site reads as one unified visual system while bespoke brand photography is commissioned.

See `docs/ASSET_READINESS.md` for the replacement briefs. These images must never be misrepresented as Baba Ayurved's own facility, products, or founders.
